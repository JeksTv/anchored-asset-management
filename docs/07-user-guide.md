# User guide

## First sign-in

Enter the username and temporary password provided by your administrator. Choose a new password when prompted, then sign in again. An email address is not required. Contact your Super Admin for a reset if you forget your password.

## Super Admin

Use Manage access for individual logins, roles, email, employee links and resets. The IT workspace has the same operational capabilities as Admin. Link the Employee User to the correct employee profile before giving access. Import reviewed data using the migration runbook or existing reviewed-import workflow. Review recent security activity after access changes.

## IT Admin

1. Find or create the employee, using the employee ID to avoid duplicates. Existing staff can be migrated with their current assignments rather than processed as new hires.
2. Record the HR request for Google Workspace and/or Microsoft 365. Carry out provisioning in the relevant provider, then record its completed status. Identical email addresses are allowed across providers; this does not make them a shared credential record.
3. Assign the laptop/desktop and accessories through the inventory/onboarding kit. Record identifying details, specifications, supplier, purchase/warranty information and condition as available.
4. Open the employee profile to review equipment, software, accounts, tasks, forms and history together.
5. Record acknowledgement, turnover or incident forms in the Forms workflow. Verify the employee and items, then review/apply the submission. Submission alone does not bypass IT review. Incident photos are private attachments. Complete required employee fields, including dates, before submission.
6. For a return unrelated to resignation, use the return/turnover workflow without marking the employee offboarded. Inspect equipment and record condition/maintenance needs.
7. For offboarding, record the departure reason and last working day. Resolve equipment returns and outstanding tasks, deactivate external accounts in Google/Microsoft and update their tracked statuses. Disable the employee's User login in Manage access; a Super Admin handles administrator logins. Open Resigned & clearance, sign the Turnover form, then sign the Clearance form using your current password and confirmation. Complete offboarding from the employee profile after both forms are signed.

Use **Manage records** to create, search, edit and delete unused employee or asset records. Records with assignment or form history cannot be deleted; preserve them for audit and use the return, retirement or offboarding workflow instead. **Manage access** lets Admins create, edit, reset, disable and delete User logins. Only Super Admins manage administrator logins and roles. Deleting a login does not delete its employee or signed documents.

Signed forms remain linked to the employee in **Resigned & clearance** and the employee profile. Filter by Resigned to distinguish resignations from other departures. View / print produces a printable document with the IT signer's identity, date, remarks and saved checklist. This is authenticated electronic sign-off, not a certificate-based digital signature. Signed forms are immutable; employee identity edits are blocked once departure documents are signed.

## Employee User

The User portal displays your linked equipment/software assignments and history, external account records, additional kit items and documents. It does not expose the company directory or other employees' records. This release's employee portal is read-only; ask IT to record a return or incident and complete forms through the existing IT-led process. Employee self-submission can be added as a separate workflow with ownership validation and approval safeguards.

If the portal says the login is not linked, ask Super Admin to link your employee profile. Adding an email alone does not establish that link.
